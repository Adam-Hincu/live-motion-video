This guide covers motion design and quality standards for creating effective animations.

1. Rules
- Every text element, screen, state, and visual must transition smoothly into the next through morphing, movement, or blending. Maintain a fluid, continuous flow without abrupt changes.
- Abrupt cuts, clipping, jitter, and poorly timed transitions, morphs, or movements are strictly unacceptable. All motion must be seamless, fluid, precisely timed, and consistent.
- Stretching is strictly prohibited.
